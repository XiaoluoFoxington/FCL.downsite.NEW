New feature: i18n support! Now it supports simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, French, Arabic and Spanish.

If you don't want warnings and errors to appear as toast notifications, you can disable them in the behavior settings.

New feature: bookmarking! Click the bookmark button in the list page or the detail page to save a resource. Saved resources will appear on the homepage and in the drawer navigation.

Enhanced system detection: if the system or version does not support the selected resource, a notice will be pinned at the top of the download page. This is for reference only and may not be perfectly accurate.
